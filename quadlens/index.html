The four lenses

Look at any lesson through these four lenses, and you'll know what to strengthen next.

1

Clarity of learning

Do students know what they are learning, and why? Learning intentions planned as Know, Understand, Do, with clear success criteria.

2

Support and challenge

Is every student stretched, and supported to succeed? High expectations, smart scaffolds, and real stretch.

3

Cognitive engagement

Who is doing the thinking? Questions that demand reasoning, every student responding, and respect for working memory.

4

Assessment for learning

How do you know they are learning, right now? Checks for understanding, responsive teaching, and feedback students use.
